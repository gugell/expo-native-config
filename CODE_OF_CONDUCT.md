# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome differing experience levels, and keep feedback focused on work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive contributions and restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately through the maintainer's verified repository contact. A public project must publish a reliable reporting channel before launch; this draft does not invent one.

These project-specific expectations are informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), without claiming to reproduce its full policy or enforcement process.
